# DEC-004 — Institutional Voice

## Status

accepted (2026-09-04)

## Decision

The user stated three requirements for how TENDER SYSTEMS presents itself:

1. TENDER SYSTEMS presents as a company or organization, not as a single author.

   > "TENDER SYSTEMS는 하나의 작가가 아니라 정말 회사나 조직 처럼 보이는게 중요해."

2. It should read as an organization on the surface while remaining a single brand underneath.

   > "조직처럼 보이지만 사실은 하나의 브랜드가 되면 좋겠어."

3. The brand is derived from the systems already built — OTHER GOODS and LONGING — rather than designed top-down before them.

   > "일단 우리가 만든 OTHER GOODS와 LONGING 에서부터 도출 되는 브랜드면 좋겠어."

The user also stated the general requirement that the identity read as corporate throughout:

> "전반적인 것이 회사의 느낌이 있어야 해."

## Rationale

Drawn only from the conversation: an organizational surface lets several systems be issued by one entity without an authorial signature. Deriving the brand from existing systems keeps it accountable to work that exists rather than to a plan.

## Consequences

- Documents are written as institutional records.
- Systems are described as operated by TENDER SYSTEMS rather than authored by a person.
- A brand framework invented before the systems it describes is out of scope.
- This decision extends, and does not replace, [[DEC-003-public-identity]], which governs the identity string used on source control.
