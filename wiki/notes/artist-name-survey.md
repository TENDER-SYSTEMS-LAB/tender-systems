# Artist Name Survey

Seven supplied LLM answers explore a personal artist pseudonym for the person making TENDER SYSTEMS. All names and rankings remain **llm-proposed**. No name, spelling, introduction, account, or exception to institutional identity has been adopted. This note compares the answers; it does not issue a new ranking.

## Scope and relationship to existing records

The answers consistently describe a real creator outside the work's world, with a name usable in Korean introductions, process videos, and later signatures. They seek a short, intelligible connection to creative motivation and durability beyond AI or a current lack of revenue. These are the answers' accounts of a shared brief: the initiating prompt and attachments are absent, so their wording is not a newly confirmed user requirement.

The direct motivation source supports spending one's own money for artistic self-realization, making work with AI about potentially disappearing human romance, and hoping to discover, reflect on, and empathize with trust, love, and understanding. [[creative-origin-and-motivation]] remains the owner of that background. The answers sometimes call it a philosophy; that does not change its recorded role or adopt their interpretations as institutional principles.

This is an **alternative under exploration**, not an evolution of the confirmed public identity. [[DEC-003-public-identity]] and [[DEC-004-institutional-voice]] still govern TENDER SYSTEMS. How a personal pseudonym could appear alongside the institution, including in process records or publication authorship, remains unresolved; see [[public-surfaces]], [[academic-guidance-and-publication]], and [[Q-002-internal-versus-public-language]].

## What the comparison adds

The answers split over how directly to retain the user's word '허비'. Grok and DeepSeek foreground expenditure and its negative everyday associations. ChatGPT shifts toward what is cherished, while Qwen shifts toward wholeness without market validation. Claude frames a choice outside calculation, Gemini favors depth of attention, and GLM joins self-funding to care through a double reading. These are competing interpretations of motivation, not interchangeable translations or evidence of a shared winner.

Recurring tradeoffs are meaning versus sounding like a person's name, Korean wordplay versus English readability, and a recognizable common word versus distinct search results. The answers mostly favor durability beyond a particular tool, but sometimes preserve a candidate by changing its interpretation if revenue appears. That flexibility is a proposal, not evidence that the name will remain comfortable in use.

Repeated candidates do not establish consensus. **진담** appears in ChatGPT's table and is Qwen's main alternative, while GLM drops it because of its reported resemblance to an earlier candidate. **오티움 / Otium** appears in both Claude and GLM without becoming either answer's final choice. Grok returns to **허비** despite describing an earlier pronunciation concern about **허비로**. The original rejection exchanges are absent; the Wiki does not turn these accounts into a confirmed rejection list.

## Evidence and verification limits

| Answer | Repository and external checking reported in the source |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Reports reading the repository and specified background documents, and supplies external links for meanings and existing uses. These reports and links were not independently verified during ingestion. |
| Claude | Explicitly reports failed repository access and reliance on the supplied brief; reports some dictionary and web searches. No handle, domain, or trademark availability check. |
| DeepSeek | Reports failed repository access and no web searching; existing uses and availability remain unknown. |
| Gemini | Reports no live external search, while marking several dictionary meanings as checked without supplying lookup evidence. Repository access is not established by the answer. |
| GLM | Explicitly reports no repository, web, or dictionary access; separates memory and general knowledge from verification. |
| Grok | Reports repository reading and web searches dated 2026-09-07, but gives only an abbreviated repository path and no specific external search citations for collision findings. |
| Qwen | Reports no repository or web access. Dictionary and etymology statements in the detailed discussion are not backed by an evidenced lookup. |

The answers' external-use findings matter to their internal rankings but remain **secondary reports**. ChatGPT reports uses affecting 아낌/AKKIM, VOLENS, SALPI, Tamyu., and DILETTO; it distinguishes exhibition-title uses of 완상 from an identical artist name. Claude reports a publisher using 다림 with the same meaning explanation and a cosmetics use of 헤아림. These are reasons those answers qualify or lower candidates, not independently verified collisions or user rejections.

No answer establishes complete domain, handle, or trademark availability. An unsuccessful search does not establish availability; Qwen's closing suggestion that finding no use would be sufficient is not adopted. Pronunciation predictions are not speaker-test results. Linguistic definitions, coined meanings, etymologies, Romanization claims, and claims of naturalness remain attributed to the source; several proposed spellings and definitions need review before reuse as factual name explanations.

The seven answers are proposal diversity, not seven independent validations: they respond to a shared background, and several explicitly could not read the repository. Their earlier naming history is secondary. The source registry records filenames, hashes, dates, and access caveats; exact model versions and original generation times are not established for every answer.

## Unresolved selection questions

- Which emphasis should a name carry: expenditure, care, wholeness, attention, seriousness, or tension?
- How should a personal pseudonym relate to the institution's confirmed public identity and language rules?
- Which candidates remain natural when spoken in an introduction and used as a signature, in both Korean and English?
- For any eventual shortlist, what do direct meaning checks, existing-use searches, and actual account or domain checks establish?
